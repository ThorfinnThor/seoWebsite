# Garden-house vertical

The planner estimates minimum storage area from bicycles, tool volume, mower, workbench and shelving. It adds a 15% movement reserve and rounds upward to 0.5 m². Bicycles or a mower raise the suggested known door width from 70 cm to 80 cm.

Hard filters apply in this order: reviewed data, physical fit (including optional rotation), sufficient area, known door width, material, roof, floor requirement, availability and budget. Unknown door width produces a warning rather than rejection. Unknown delivery cost produces an unknown budget status rather than being treated as free.

The planner does not determine permission, setbacks, structure, wind/snow load or foundation design. The available dimensions entered by the user must already represent a usable and permitted footprint.
